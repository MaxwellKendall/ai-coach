## 12-week probe: The author: 3 days, gym, build week

Logged like a person: about one session in four skipped, and every third week the main lifts' last set a rep short at RPE 9.5.

Brief by claude-haiku-5-5:

- **base**: Volume and movement quality; build squat and bench work capacity with higher reps. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Barbell Row, Pull-up, 8 reps; the rest 12.
- **build**: Heavier loads to convert base volume into strength on squat and bench. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Barbell Shoulder Press, 5 reps; the rest 8.
- **peak**: Maximal strength and test-week readiness for the 225 bench and 275 squat goals. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, 3 reps; the rest 6.
