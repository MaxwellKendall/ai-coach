## 12-week probe: The author: 3 days, gym, build week

Logged like a person: about one session in four skipped, and every third week the main lifts' last set a rep short at RPE 9.5.

Brief by claude-haiku-5-5:

- **base**: Volume and movement quality: build muscle and groove squat and bench technique. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Barbell Row, Pull-up, 8 reps; the rest 12.
- **build**: Heavier loading to raise the squat and bench estimated maxes. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Standing Military Press, Bent Over Barbell Row, 5 reps; the rest 8.
- **peak**: Heaviest work with low reps to express strength for the Dec 1 test. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Barbell Row, 3 reps; the rest 5.
