## 12-week probe: The author: 3 days, gym, build week

Logged like a person: about one session in four skipped, and every third week the main lifts' last set a rep short at RPE 9.5.

Brief by claude-haiku-5-5:

- **base**: Volume and movement quality: build muscle and groove squat and bench technique at moderate loads. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Barbell Row, 10 reps; the rest 12.
- **build**: Strength: heavier loads to push squat and bench numbers toward 275 and 225. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Barbell Row, 6 reps; the rest 8.
- **peak**: Peak: heaviest loads with low reps to prepare for the week 12 test on squat and bench. Main lifts: Back Squat, Bench Press, 3 reps; the rest 6.
