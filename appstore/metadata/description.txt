barbrabell is for people who are coached.

Your coach writes your week. You open the app, see today's session, and log each set as you go. Your coach sees what you did and adjusts what comes next.

Today — your session, ready to start. Nothing to set up.
Log in seconds — check off a lift, or open the session and log each set. The weights and reps your coach planned are already filled in.
Runs — track a run with GPS and a map, saved to Apple Health.
Check in — weight, sleep and a note your coach reads.
Progress — your lifts over time, worked out from what you actually logged.

Not coached yet? The app still works on its own. Tap "Train on my own" on the first screen, pick a ready-made program, and log your training, runs and check-ins without an account.

Coaches can open My Clients in the app to see who needs them, reply, and change a session. Programming tools and billing stay on the web.

barbrabell is free. There is nothing to buy in the app and there are no ads.
