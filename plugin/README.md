# Barbrabell for Claude

Coach your strength clients from Claude. Write a client's program in a conversation, see who needs you this week, and bring in programs you already have in a spreadsheet or PDF. Every change lands in your client's free Barbrabell iPhone app, where they follow the sessions and log each set; you see how it went here and on the dashboard at [barbrabell.com/app](https://barbrabell.com/app).

## What's in it

- **The Barbrabell connector** (`https://barbrabell.com/mcp`): your clients' goals, programs, planned sessions and logs, with tools to plan, review and reply. Nothing changes on a client's phone until you say yes.
- **Skills** Claude uses when you ask:
  - **Onboard a client**: invite them, fill in what you know, and set up their program and first two weeks.
  - **Program a block**: lay out a multi-week block for a client and plan its first weeks.
  - **Plan next week**: write a client's coming week from the program and how this week went.
  - **Roster review**: who needs you this week, with replies to their session notes for you to approve.
  - **Bring in a program**: turn a client's existing program (a sheet, CSV, PDF or screenshot) into their program here.

## Set up

1. Sign in at [barbrabell.com/app](https://barbrabell.com/app) and start the coach plan.
2. Install this plugin in Claude (Customize › Plugins), then connect Barbrabell when Claude asks and sign in with the same Google or Apple account.
3. Ask Claude something like "Invite a new client" or "Who needs me this week?".

Your clients install Barbrabell from the App Store and join you from your invite link or its 6-character code.
